## Brand & Style

This design system drives high-tempo logistics, on-demand field routing, and parcel integrity verification. Tailored specifically for fast-moving courier personnel and delivery fleets, the visual narrative balances tactical precision with approachable, optimistic consumer energy.

### Design Principles & Aesthetic
- **Tactical Speed & Optimism:** Blending high-chroma magenta with Anteraja's energetic yellow (`#FFCF0E`) infuses the system with velocity, warmth, and unmistakable brand presence.
- **Glanceable Utility:** Built around rapid motorcycle navigation, ambient sunlight readability, and single-handed gloved touch ergonomics.
- **Verification Confidence:** Proof-of-delivery (PoD), barcode scanning, and status milestones demand high-fidelity clarity to eliminate delivery failure and cognitive fatigue.
- **Visual Style:** Modern logistics-minimalism infused with high-contrast alert tones, deliberate structural cards, and crisp micro-interactions.

## Colors

The color palette centers on the signature electric magenta (`#E00065`) coupled with Anteraja energetic yellow (`#FFCF0E`), creating an unmistakable high-visibility harmony tailored for rapid identification in logistics workflows.

### Color Roles & Hierarchy
- **Primary (`#E00065`):** The primary operational anchor. Governs primary action buttons, key operational confirmations, active navigational tabs, and progress tracks.
- **Secondary (`#FFCF0E`):** Energetic yellow accents express movement, real-time urgency, expedited parcel badges, and attention-critical alerts.
- **Tertiary (`#00834b`):** Deep verification emerald. Dedicated strictly to successful delivery confirmations, geo-fence drop verification, digital signature approval, and completed manifest tallies.
- **Neutral (`#1C1B1B`):** Deep carbon charcoal ensuring sharp contrast on bright days and eliminating washed-out text under roadside glare.
- **Surface Strategy:** Luminous, neutral canvas surfaces (`#FCF9F8`) stack under crisp white cards (`#FFFFFF`) framed by disciplined dividers (`#EAE7E7`).

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans brings welcoming geometric geometry paired with substantial x-height and open counters. This typographic profile prevents legibility failures during high vibration or outdoor screen reflection.

### Structural Typographic Rules
- **AWB & Serial Codes (`barcode-tracking`):** Enhanced character tracking (+0.08em) prevents couriers from confusing `0`, `O`, `8`, and `B` during manual cross-checks.
- **Operational Metrics & Stop Numbers:** Rendered in bold/extrabold headlines (`headline-md`, `headline-lg`) with snug negative letter spacing for immediate glanceability.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** High-contrast, tight, uppercase-capable weights provide distinct scannable headers for address blocks, recipient phone numbers, and COD billing flags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is optimized for high thumb accessibility on handheld mobile hardware, organizing complex logistics stops into clean, repeatable modules.

### Grid & Density Rules
- **Mobile Grid Structure:** 4-column responsive layout utilizing a `1rem` outer margin and `0.75rem` column gutter.
- **Thumb Danger & Reach Zones:** The lower 88px to 104px of the viewport is protected for sticky actions: scanner triggers, swipe-to-deliver docks, and call shortcuts.
- **Component Padding Rhythms:**
  - Standard parcel cards and manifest items leverage `space-md` (0.75rem) to ensure maximum items per viewport without cramped touch targets.
  - Detail panels, parcel breakdowns, and proof-of-delivery summaries use `space-lg` (1rem) internal padding.

## Elevation & Depth

Tactile physical cues in sunlight depend on crisp edge delineation and low-blur high-opacity offsets over diffuse shadows.

### Elevation Layers
- **Ground Floor (Base Canvas):** Flat `#FCF9F8` tint reducing white reflection strain on eyes outdoors.
- **Level 1 (Parcel Cards & Form Modules):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a crisp 1px `#EAE7E7` perimeter stroke and a clean elevation shadow: `0px 2px 4px rgba(28, 27, 27, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Active/Selected Stop):** Active parcels gain priority with a warm energetic amber/yellow edge indicator or magenta outline, accompanied by `0px 8px 18px rgba(224, 0, 101, 0.12)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Actions & Drawers):** Barcode scanner FABs and sliding bottom drawers sit highest above the route scroll with `0px -4px 20px rgba(28, 27, 27, 0.14)`.

## Shapes

The interface balances crisp functional discipline with accessible, friendly corner geometry using standard `rounded-md` tokens (8px base, scaling to 12px and 16px for larger containment).

### Corner Application Standards
- **Task & Parcel Cards:** Standardized on `0.5rem` (8px) to `0.75rem` (12px) for structured spatial packing without wasting screen real estate.
- **Primary Buttons & Form Inputs:** Crisp 12px corners for comfortable, glove-friendly touch targets.
- **Status Pills & Counters:** Retain fully pill-shaped caps (`9999px`) to create clear visual separation between structural layout containers and interactive status indicators.
- **Bottom Drawers:** 16px to 24px top radii for welcoming, ergonomic bottom-sheet gestures.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive CTAs
- **Primary Action (Delivery Completion):**
  - High-impact magenta `#E00065` fill with bold white typography, 52px touch height.
  - Secondary quick-action buttons utilize energetic yellow `#FFCF0E` with dark `#1C1B1B` typography for attention-grabbing interim steps (e.g., "Scan Return", "Expedite Route").
  - Pressed states shift magenta toward deep wine and energetic yellow toward deep goldenrod.

### Badges & Status Chips
- **Urgent / Expedited / Sameday:** Energetic yellow `#FFCF0E` background with `#1C1B1B` bold text.
- **Delivered / Verified:** Tertiary green tint `#E6F6EC` with `#00834B` text and solid checkmark.
- **Out for Delivery / In Transit:** Soft magenta tint `#FFF0F5` with `#E00065` text.
- **Failed / Rescheduled:** High-visibility amber with dark contrasting labels.

### Parcel Manifest Cards
- High-contrast white surfaces framed by 1px subtle borders. Left border highlights an active 4px accent bar: energetic yellow `#FFCF0E` for express stops, and `#E00065` for default priority.
- Prominent tracking number rendered with `barcode-tracking` style for effortless glanceability.
- Integrated quick-action icon buttons (Call, Map Route, Copy AWB) with minimum 44px hit bounds.

### Input Fields & Verification Controls
- Form inputs feature a 52px height, 1.5px neutral border (`#EAE7E7`), and a sharp `#E00065` focus boundary with an energetic yellow corner indicator tag for required fields.
- Signature Pad framed in clean `#FFFFFF` with clear baselines and a high-visibility tertiary check confirm button.
- Camera PoD (Proof of Delivery) viewfinder framed with a responsive bounding reticle and instant thumbnail verification chip.
